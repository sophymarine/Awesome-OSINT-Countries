# Awesome OSINT — Seychelles 🇸🇨

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge-flat2.svg)](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome)

> Company search, beneficial ownership and court search resources for Seychelles.

Seychelles is one of the highest-volume International Business Company jurisdictions in the world and one of the thinnest for public corporate data. The registry will confirm that a company exists and what its status is; historically it has disclosed little about shareholders or directors, and there is no public beneficial ownership search at all.

The compensating resource is **SeyLII**, a free and well-maintained legal information institute that puts Supreme Court, Constitutional Court and Court of Appeal judgments online at no cost — considerably better court access than the corporate access.

## Company registry — official

- [Seychelles Financial Services Authority](https://fsaseychelles.sc/) — the FSA is both the company registrar for International Business Companies and the non-bank financial regulator. The single official body for Seychelles corporate research.
- [FSA — IBC legislation and resources](https://fsaseychelles.sc/component/edocman/legislations/fiduciary/ibc) — the International Business Companies Act 2016 and associated material.

A Seychelles company search returns an extract issued under the IBC Act 2016 confirming current company details and status. An online registry exists, but it has **historically provided limited information about shareholders or directors**, and there is no rich free public search comparable to Cyprus or Panama.

## Regulated entities

- [Seychelles Financial Services Authority](https://fsaseychelles.sc/) — licensed fiduciaries, securities dealers, insurance businesses, fund administrators and international trade zone operators. Licensee lists and public notices are published here and are free — as elsewhere offshore, the regulator often discloses more than the registrar.

## Beneficial ownership

- [Beneficial Ownership — Seychelles Financial Intelligence Unit](https://seychellesfiu.sc/beneficial-ownership/) — the official page on the regime.

The **Financial Intelligence Unit** is the nodal agency maintaining the Seychelles beneficial ownership database. Beneficial ownership particulars are submitted **electronically by the resident agent within 14 days** of a person becoming a beneficial owner, for the legal persons and legal arrangements the agent administers.

**There is no public beneficial ownership search.** No BO search function appears on the FSA's public search surfaces, and the FIU database is not open to general enquiry.

- [Open Ownership — jurisdiction map](https://www.openownership.org/en/map/) — comparative position and any change in access.

## Courts — judgments and case law

- [SeyLII — Seychelles Legal Information Institute](https://seylii.org/) — **the primary free legal resource.** An independent association established in 2012 under the auspices of the Judiciary of Seychelles and supported by AfricanLII. Free and anonymous access to case law, legislation and secondary legal materials: consolidated legislation, statutory instruments, Acts as enacted, and judgments of the **Supreme Court, Constitutional Court and Court of Appeal**.
- [SeyLII — Supreme Court decisions (legacy archive)](https://old.seylii.org/sc/judgments/supreme-court/h) — the older SeyLII interface, which still holds material that can be easier to browse by court and year.
- [The Judiciary of Seychelles — Court decisions](https://www.judiciary.sc/resources/court-decisions/) — the official judiciary's decisions page.
- [The Judiciary of Seychelles — Access to information](https://www.judiciary.sc/resources/freedom-of-information/) — what the judiciary will release and how to ask.

Court of Appeal decisions are available free online, either as complete downloadable PDF volumes or browsable decision by decision.

## Practical notes

**The IBC is the whole game.** Seychelles IBCs are incorporated in volume, cheaply and quickly, through licensed resident agents. They are designed for minimal public disclosure. Expect an extract to establish existence, name, number and status, and little else.

**The resident agent is the only reliable structural handle.** Every IBC must have a Seychelles resident agent, who holds the beneficial ownership record and files it to the FIU. Identifying the agent identifies the service provider, and agents cluster heavily — a handful of firms administer a very large share of the register.

**Company names recur across jurisdictions.** Seychelles IBCs are frequently one leg of a multi-jurisdiction structure, with near-identical names registered in the BVI, Belize, Panama or the Marshall Islands. A name match is not an entity match; confirm the registration number and jurisdiction.

**Check the courts for the corporate detail.** SeyLII judgments — particularly commercial, insolvency and asset-recovery matters — name shareholders, directors and underlying structures that the registry does not. Search by company name, by suspected principal, and by resident agent.

**Mixed legal system.** Seychelles law blends French civil law (the Civil Code) with English common law procedure. Judgments cite both traditions and use French-derived terminology in substantive private law. English is the working language of the courts.

**Two SeyLII interfaces.** The current site and the legacy `old.seylii.org` archive do not present identical results. If a search comes up empty on one, try the other before concluding the judgment is not online.

**Verify against the FSA.** Commercial intermediaries sell Seychelles company search reports; the authoritative extract is the FSA's, issued under the IBC Act. For anything consequential, insist on the official extract.
