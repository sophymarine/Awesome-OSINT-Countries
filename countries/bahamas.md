# Awesome OSINT — The Bahamas 🇧🇸

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge-flat2.svg)](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome)

> Company search, beneficial ownership and court search resources for The Bahamas.

The Bahamas charges more per company search than almost any comparable jurisdiction — around BSD 100 a query, with no free tier and no preview — while publishing its appellate judgments online for nothing. That asymmetry defines Bahamian research: the corporate layer is expensive and shallow, and the court layer is free and genuinely useful.

Beneficial ownership follows the regional pattern: collected through registered agents, held in a non-public system, visible only to designated authorities.

## Company registry — official

- [Registrar General's Department](https://www.rgd.gov.bs/) — the statutory registry, within the Ministry of Finance. Operates from the Bahamas Financial Centre, Shirley and Charlotte Streets, Nassau.
- [RGD — Online Company Search](https://www.rgd.gov.bs/service/company-search) — the public search service.
- [Bahamas Corporate Registry](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/corporate-registry) — the government portal's registry pages.
- [Registry of Companies legislation](https://www.rgd.gov.bs/registry-of-companies-legislation) — the statutory basis and the forms.

Searches run through the **CARS** platform (Corporate Administrative Registry Services). A company search costs around **BSD 100** per query, payable by card, and does **not** require a registered account. The Bahamian dollar is pegged 1:1 to the US dollar.

## Regulated entities

- [Securities Commission of The Bahamas](https://www.scb.gov.bs/) — registered firms, investment funds, financial and corporate service providers, and digital asset businesses. Public registers and enforcement notices, free. The Bahamas is a significant funds and digital-asset domicile, so this register covers a large share of the entities that matter.
- [Central Bank of The Bahamas](https://www.centralbankbahamas.com/) — licensed banks and trust companies.

## Beneficial ownership

The **Register of Beneficial Ownership Act, 2018** came into force on 20 December 2018. All Bahamian companies and international business companies must provide prescribed beneficial ownership information to their **registered agents** and update it within **15 days** of any change.

The data is held in a **non-public registry, accessible only to designated authorities.** There is no public search and no legitimate-interest route.

- [The Register of Beneficial Ownership Act — Office of the Attorney General](https://www.oag.gov.bs/notices/the-register-of-beneficial-ownership-act) — the official notice and statutory material.
- [Register of Beneficial Ownership — Registrar General's Department](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/corporate-registry) — the registry's guidance.
- [Open Ownership — jurisdiction map](https://www.openownership.org/en/map/) — comparative position.

## Courts — judgments and case law

- [Court of Appeal of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas — Judgments](https://www.courtofappeal.org.bs/judgments.php) — **free and searchable.** An up-to-date listing of recent judgments plus an archive of judgments handed down by the Court. The Court of Appeal is the highest court resident in The Bahamas, so this is the most substantial free Bahamian legal database.
- [Court of Appeal of The Bahamas](https://www.courtofappeal.org.bs/) — the Court's main site.
- [The Judiciary of The Bahamas — Courts](https://courts.bs/courts/) — court structure and the Supreme Court, which has unlimited original jurisdiction in civil and criminal matters and hears appeals from the Magistrates' Courts.
- [The Judicature — Bahamas court system and judges](https://www.bahamas.gov.bs/the-judicature) — official overview of the court hierarchy.
- [Judicial Committee of the Privy Council](https://www.jcpc.uk/) — final court of appeal for The Bahamas.

## Legislation

- [Laws of The Bahamas](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/) — consolidated statutes, free. The Companies Act (Ch. 308), the International Business Companies Act and the Register of Beneficial Ownership Act 2018 are all here.

## Practical notes

**Search before you search.** At around BSD 100 a query with no preview, a wrong or imprecise company name is an expensive mistake. Pin the exact registered name from a free source first — a court judgment, a Securities Commission register entry, a regulatory notice, or a filing in another jurisdiction that names the Bahamian entity — then pay once.

**IBCs versus domestic companies.** The Bahamas runs two tracks: domestic companies under the Companies Act and **international business companies** under the IBC Act. IBCs are the offshore vehicle and carry lighter public disclosure. Establish which regime an entity sits under before drawing conclusions from thin results.

**The registered agent is the pivot.** As throughout the region, every IBC has a registered agent who holds the beneficial ownership information. The agent is the structural fingerprint, and agent identity is often recoverable from court filings even when it is not cheap to get from the registry.

**Free regulators, paid registrar.** The Securities Commission and Central Bank registers cost nothing and cover funds, financial and corporate service providers, banks and trust companies. For a large share of Bahamian entities of interest, you can establish existence and regulatory status without paying the Registrar at all.

**The Court of Appeal archive is underused.** It is free, it is searchable by party, and Bahamian commercial and insolvency appeals routinely name the corporate structures behind a dispute. When the registry is too expensive to fish in, fish here.

**Privy Council for the big matters.** Significant Bahamian commercial disputes end at the JCPC, whose judgments are free, thorough and fully public.
