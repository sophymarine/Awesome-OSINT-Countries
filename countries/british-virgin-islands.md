# Awesome OSINT — British Virgin Islands 🇻🇬

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge-flat2.svg)](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome)

> Company search, beneficial ownership and court search resources for the BVI.

The BVI is the highest-volume offshore company domicile in the world and one of the least searchable. There is no free public company search. Beneficial ownership sits in a system explicitly designed to be queryable by authorities and by nobody else. The registry will confirm that a company exists and is in good standing, and very little more.

What is open is the court. The BVI Commercial Court, part of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court, hears a large share of the world's offshore shareholder and insolvency disputes, and its judgments name the structures that the registry will not. As with Cayman: **research the BVI through its litigation.**

## Company registry — official

- [BVI Financial Services Commission](https://www.bvifsc.vg/) — the FSC is both the registry and the financial regulator. The single official body for BVI corporate research.
- [BVI FSC — Public Search and BO Search](https://www.bvifsc.vg/public-search) — the FSC's search entry point, covering the public registry search and the separate (restricted) beneficial ownership route.
- **VIRRGIN** — the Virtual Integrated Registry and Regulatory General Information Network, the FSC's registry system holding incorporation and standing data. Access is intermediated through licensed registered agents rather than offered as an open public portal.

A registry extract discloses the company **name, registration number, status, incorporation date and registered agent**. It does not disclose directors, shareholders or beneficial owners.

- [companysearch.vg](https://companysearch.vg/) — commercial BVI search intermediary. Independent of the FSC; useful when you cannot go through a registered agent, but verify anything material against the official record.

## Regulated entities

The FSC regulates BVI banks, trust and company service providers, insurers, investment business licensees and funds. Licensee lists and regulatory enforcement notices are published on the [FSC site](https://www.bvifsc.vg/) — worth checking separately from the company register, because the regulatory file often carries names the registry does not.

## Beneficial ownership

- **BOSS — the Beneficial Ownership Secure Search System.** Established by the BOSS Act 2017 and its amendments. BVI business companies maintain beneficial ownership information through their licensed registered agents, filed into the secure system.

**Access.** BOSS data is not public. It has historically been accessible to **BVI and UK competent authorities** only. From **April 2026**, a legitimate-interest access route opened: parties may request access, but **the beneficial owner is notified of the request and may object.** That notification provision is significant for investigative work — a legitimate-interest request is not a silent one.

- [Open Ownership — jurisdiction map](https://www.openownership.org/en/map/) — track the current state of the BVI regime; the access rules have moved repeatedly and will move again.

## Courts — judgments and case law

- [Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court](https://www.eccourts.org/) — the ECSC is the superior court for the BVI and eight other OECS states and territories. Its site carries High Court judgments, Court of Appeal judgments, digests of decisions, and sitting schedules. Free.
- **BVI Commercial Court** — a division of the ECSC High Court sitting in Tortola, established 2009, handling shareholder disputes, unfair-prejudice petitions, liquidations and cross-border insolvency. Its judgments are the richest open source on BVI corporate structures. Search the ECSC judgments by party name.
- [Judicial Committee of the Privy Council](https://www.jcpc.uk/) — final court of appeal for the BVI. Free, fully public, well-indexed, and frequently the most detailed factual account of a long-running offshore dispute.

## Courts — filings and cause lists

- [ECSC E-Litigation Portal — user information](https://www.eccourts.org/ecsc-e-litigation-portal-user-information) — the electronic filing and case management system covering all ECSC courts. The BVI was in the first implementation phase alongside Saint Lucia and Anguilla, and was the first jurisdiction to implement the criminal module. Filing access is for practitioners; the portal also underpins the published sittings and case information.

## Practical notes

**There is no free company search.** Plan for a registered agent, a commercial intermediary, or a paid FSC product. Do not assume an absence of results means an absence of company.

**The registered agent is the most useful field you can get.** Every BVI company must have a licensed registered agent, and the agent is disclosed on the registry extract. Agents cluster: identifying the agent tells you which service provider built the structure, and the same agent will appear across related vehicles. When you cannot see owners, map agents.

**Directors and shareholders are not on the public record.** A register of directors is filed with the Registrar but is not publicly searchable; the register of members is company-held. Neither is obtainable through open search.

**Company numbers are sequential and dateable.** BVI registration numbers run in series over time, so a number gives a rough incorporation era even before you see the date. Useful for sanity-checking a claimed incorporation date.

**Names are recycled and struck-off companies restored.** A BVI company can be struck off, remain in limbo, and later be restored. "Struck off" is not "dissolved" and is not permanent — check status and status date together, and re-check later in a live matter.

**The court is the way in.** If registry research dead-ends — which it usually does — search the ECSC and Privy Council judgment databases for the company name, the registered agent, the suspected principals, and any affiliated entity in a jurisdiction with a more open register. Offshore litigation is the leakiest part of the system.

**Watch the legitimate-interest notification rule.** Under the 2026 access route the beneficial owner is told who asked. Factor that into the sequencing of any live investigation.
