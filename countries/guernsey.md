> Company search, beneficial ownership and court search resources for the Bailiwick of Guernsey.

Guernsey is the quieter of the two main Channel Island financial centres, and in one respect the more open: the Registry states plainly that its duty includes making **some** beneficial ownership information publicly available — a formulation you will not find in Jersey, the BVI or Cayman. The court archive, run jointly by the Royal Court and the Law Officers of the Crown, is free and unusually deep for a jurisdiction of this size.

## Company registry — official

- [Guernsey Registry](https://www.guernseyregistry.com/) — the statutory registry. Its online portal holds the **company, limited partnership, limited liability partnership, foundation and charity/NPO registers**, and supports both searching and submissions. That spread matters: Guernsey foundations and LPs are common structuring vehicles and sit in the same searchable system as companies.
- [Guernsey Registry — search](https://guernseyregistry.com/article/2987/Search) — the search entry point.

## Regulated entities

- [Guernsey Financial Services Commission](https://www.gfsc.gg/) — licensed banks, insurers, investment firms, fiduciaries and fund administrators, plus public enforcement notices. Separate from the Registry; check both.

## Beneficial ownership

- [Beneficial Ownership Registers — Guernsey Registry](https://www.guernseyregistry.com/article/120012/Beneficial-Ownership-Registers) — the Registry's page on the regime.
- [Beneficial Ownership guidance](https://www.guernseyregistry.com/beneficialownership) — filing obligations and the nominated-person process.
- [Beneficial Ownership (Definition) Regulations, 2017](https://www.guernseylegalresources.gg/CHttpHandler.ashx?documentid=79261) — the statutory definition of a beneficial owner.

The **Beneficial Ownership of Legal Persons (Guernsey) Law, 2017** came into force on 15 August 2017. Since then all Guernsey companies must file beneficial ownership information. Filings are made through a **"nominated person"** submission in the Online Services Portal, with certified proof of identity and address uploaded alongside.

The Registrar's stated duty is to receive and retain basic information about legal entities and their beneficial owners **and to make some of that information publicly available**. Treat that as the ceiling rather than the floor: the full beneficial ownership register is not open, and what surfaces publicly is the basic entity information. Check the current position on the Registry pages above rather than assuming — Guernsey's disclosure settings have moved with international pressure.

## Courts — judgments and case law

- [Guernsey Legal Resources](https://www.guernseylegalresources.gg/) — **the primary free legal database**, a joint initiative of the Royal Court of Guernsey and the Law Officers of the Crown. Holds:
  - Orders of the Royal Court from **1949** onwards
  - Practice Directions of the Royal Court from **2000** onwards
  - Unreported judgments of the Guernsey courts from **2003** onwards
  - Guernsey Law Reports from **2003** onwards
  - Consolidated legislation
- [Royal Court of Guernsey](https://www.guernseyroyalcourt.gg/) — court information, sittings and procedure. The Royal Court sits as the Ordinary Court and the Full Court, with Jurats as judges of fact alongside the Bailiff.
- [Judicial Committee of the Privy Council](https://www.jcpc.uk/) — final court of appeal for Guernsey.

## Practical notes

**The Bailiwick is not just Guernsey.** The Bailiwick of Guernsey includes **Alderney and Sark**, which have their own registries and legal arrangements. Alderney in particular has a separate gambling regulator (the Alderney Gambling Control Commission) and its own company register. A "Guernsey" entity may be an Alderney entity — check which island.

**Foundations and LPs sit in the same system.** Unlike many jurisdictions where foundations are opaque or separately administered, the Guernsey Registry covers foundations, LPs and LLPs in the same searchable portal. If a structure uses a Guernsey foundation, that is more tractable than the equivalent in Liechtenstein or Panama.

**Trusts are not registered.** As in Jersey, Guernsey trusts have no public register. A Guernsey company acting as trustee reveals nothing about the trust behind it.

**Customary law and Norman-French terms.** Guernsey law descends from Norman customary law. Expect *Jurats*, *Bailiff*, *Ordinary Court*, *Full Court*, *saisie* and similar terms in the court record.

**Judgment coverage begins in 2003.** For unreported judgments and Law Reports, 2003 is the practical floor. Royal Court **Orders** go back to 1949, which is a different and often overlooked record — orders can establish the existence and shape of older proceedings where no judgment text survives online.
