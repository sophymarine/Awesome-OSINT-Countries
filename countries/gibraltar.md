# Awesome OSINT — Gibraltar 🇬🇮

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge-flat2.svg)](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome)

> Company search, beneficial ownership and court search resources for Gibraltar.

Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory with a company registry that holds more than most offshore registers — **directors, secretaries and shareholders are all on the register** — and charges for access to all of it. There is no free public company search. Beneficial ownership is held by licensed service providers and fed to a secure central database that the public cannot query.

For a jurisdiction heavily used in online gaming, insurance and, more recently, distributed-ledger business, the regulator's registers are often more informative than the company registry.

## Company registry — official

- [Companies House Gibraltar](https://www.companieshouse.gi/) — the statutory registry.
- [Companies House Gibraltar — e-Registry](https://www.companieshouse.gi/login.html) — the online search and filing service. Documents held in the registers are available online **via paid subscription**; there is no free public search.
- [Companies House — Government of Gibraltar](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/business/companies-house) — the government's guidance pages on registry services and fees.

Registry data includes company name, registration number, status and address, **directors and company secretaries, and shareholder names and shareholdings** — a wider disclosure set than the BVI, Cayman, Bermuda or the Channel Islands offer, once you have paid to see it.

## Regulated entities

- [Gibraltar Financial Services Commission](https://www.fsc.gi/) — licensed banks, insurers, insurance managers, investment firms, funds, and DLT providers, with public registers and enforcement notices. Gibraltar was an early mover on distributed-ledger technology licensing, so the GFSC register covers entities that have no meaningful footprint elsewhere.
- [Gibraltar Gambling Division / licensing](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/) — Gibraltar is a major online gambling licensing jurisdiction; the list of licensed operators is public and is often the fastest way to connect a consumer-facing brand to a Gibraltar corporate entity.

## Beneficial ownership

Under the **Beneficial Ownership Act 2019**, Gibraltar companies must maintain beneficial ownership registers, held by licensed company service providers and submitted to a **secure central database**.

**Access is restricted** to law enforcement, the GFSC and other competent authorities. **There is no public search function.**

- [Open Ownership — jurisdiction map](https://www.openownership.org/en/map/) — comparative state of play and any change in Gibraltar's access model.

## Courts and legislation

- [Gibraltar Laws](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/) — the consolidated legislation of Gibraltar, free. The Companies Act and the Beneficial Ownership Act 2019 are here.
- [Government of Gibraltar](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/) — the route into the courts service, the Supreme Court Registry and justice-related departments.
- **Supreme Court Registry** — handles filings and registry enquiries for civil, criminal, family and admiralty matters. Court records are obtained through the Registry rather than an online judgment database; there is no free, comprehensive public judgment search comparable to Jersey's or the Isle of Man's.
- [Judicial Committee of the Privy Council](https://www.jcpc.uk/) — final court of appeal for Gibraltar, free and fully public.

Gibraltar's superior court judgments are less systematically published online than those of the Crown Dependencies. For significant commercial matters, the Privy Council record and the parties' own filings in other jurisdictions are often the more productive route.

## Practical notes

**Shareholders are on the register — and that is unusual.** Most offshore registers stop at existence and status. Gibraltar's includes shareholders and shareholdings. If a structure routes through Gibraltar, that link in the chain is likely to be the one that names people, provided the search budget is there.

**But nominees still apply.** Registered shareholders in Gibraltar are frequently nominee companies supplied by a licensed service provider. Registered ownership is not beneficial ownership, and the beneficial layer is exactly the part held in the non-public database.

**Everything costs.** Budget for an e-Registry subscription. Unlike Cyprus or Jersey, there is no free tier that will confirm even basic company details.

**Gambling and DLT licensing is the open flank.** Operator licence lists, regulatory announcements and enforcement notices are public and name entities and often individuals. For Gibraltar's two signature industries, the regulator publishes more than the registrar.

**EU exit changed the legal frame.** Gibraltar left the EU with the UK. Material predating 2021 may reference EU passporting arrangements that no longer apply — relevant when interpreting a company's stated regulatory basis in older filings.
